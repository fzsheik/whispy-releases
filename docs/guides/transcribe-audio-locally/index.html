How to transcribe audio locally on Mac and Windows

Whispy turns an audio file you already have into a transcript on your computer. Audio does not upload to Whispy. After the speech model is downloaded, it works offline.

This is the desktop path for the current release, v0.1.11. Download and purchase links go to usewhispy.com. Checked 25 November 2026.

  1. Confirm the computer is supported

    Use an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) on macOS 11 or newer, or Windows 10 or 11 on a 64-bit Intel or AMD PC. Intel Macs and Linux are not supported. The download page offers a Mac .dmg and a Windows x64 .exe. Leave about 2 GB free for the speech model the desktop setup downloads.

  2. Download Whispy Desktop from usewhispy.com

    Open https://usewhispy.com/download and get the current installer. The latest published release is v0.1.11 (17 November 2026). This GitHub Pages site does not host the installer.

  3. Let the first launch download the speech model

    The first run needs a network connection to download the speech model. After that download, transcription works offline. The homepage says that once you start using Whispy, you don't even need an internet connection.

  4. Choose a speed and a language model

    Pick a fast transcription for a quick review, or a highly accurate transcription for detailed notes. The v0.1.11 changelog says you pick the model for your language in settings: Parakeet Ultra covers 25 European languages, and Qwen3-ASR is for Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and more. Custom words under Advanced work with Whisper and Qwen3-ASR.

  5. Drop in the recording

    Drag and drop a voice memo, meeting recording, or interview file into the app. Version 0.1.11 can also take files from a phone: choose Send from phone, scan the QR code, and the files go to Whispy over your own Wi-Fi, not through the cloud. Free sends one file at a time. Pro sends a batch.

  6. Read, copy, or export the transcript

    Review the text, copy what you need, or export plain TXT or SRT. Transcripts and notes stay on your hard drive. You can also export the text into tools you already use. The 0.1.11 changelog says a transcript can go to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini with your own instructions, while transcription itself stays on the computer and you choose what gets shared.

Optional: Desktop Pro

Core transcription, the transcript library, notes, and TXT and SRT export are free. Desktop Pro is $12 per month, $8 per month billed annually ($96 per year), or $79 once for Pro Lifetime. Pro adds batch processing, speaker detection, speaker editing, auto-export, and transcription from a YouTube or social media link. There is no free trial. Payments are processed by Polar. See pricing.

If you cannot install the desktop app

Linux and Intel Macs do not have a desktop build. The free browser page at usewhispy.com/upload transcribes without an account and without uploading the audio. The speech model runs in the browser tab. The drop zone lists MP3, WAV, M4A, and FLAC, up to 50 MB. The first use downloads a speech model, about 150 MB for the default Tiny model.

The app’s auto-updater reads release files in the whispy-releases GitHub releases, including latest.json. Those files are not copied onto this site. Install from usewhispy.com/download.

Short answers are on the FAQ. Version notes are on the releases page.
